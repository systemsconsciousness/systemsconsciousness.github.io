Mountains and Waters

' + excerpt + '

In systems inquiry, the novice sees isolated components (reductionism). In intermediate inquiry, all boundaries dissolve into an undifferentiated sea of interconnectedness (holism).

In true systems consciousness—the third stage of Zen realization—boundaries and organs reappear. Not as isolated absolutes, but as relational membranes in dynamic equilibrium.

Form is emptiness; emptiness is form. The mountain stands solid precisely because it is fluidly continuous with the rain, the mantle, and the sky.
