    Interbeing and Systems Wholeness

    "If you are a poet, you will see clearly that there is a cloud floating in this sheet of paper."

    Thich Nhat Hanh coined the word Interbeing to express the Buddhist principle of Pratītyasamutpāda (dependent co-arising):

    Without a cloud, there will be no rain; without rain, the trees cannot grow; and without trees, we cannot make paper. The cloud is essential for the paper to exist. If the cloud is not here, the sheet of paper cannot be here either. So we can say that the cloud and the paper inter-are.

    In modern systems dynamics, this is the foundational truth of stock-and-flow networks: every stock is an accumulation of flowing relationships with the whole biosphere.
