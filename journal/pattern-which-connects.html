The Pattern Which Connects

' + excerpt + '

Gregory Bateson in Mind and Nature argued that the foundational pathology of human thought is dividing the creature from its environment.

Mind is not located inside the cranium. Mind is the total recursive feedback circuit that flows through the creature, its physical environment, its motor actions, and its sensory reflections.

When you sever an ecological relationship, you do not damage an external object—you sever part of the neural circuitry of your own systemic anatomy.
