    Gall's Law & The Tao of Complexity

    "A complex system that works is invariably found to have evolved from a simple system that worked."

    John Gall formulated the golden rule of systems engineering:

    A complex system designed from scratch never works and cannot be patched up to make it work. You have to start over with a working simple system.

    This mirrors Chapter 64 of the Tao Te Ching:

    A tree that can fill the span of a man's arms grows from a tiny sprout. A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step. Tackle the difficult while it is still easy; achieve great things through the smallest actions.

    Evolutionary adaptation, modularity, and quiet patience defeat top-down grandiosity every single time.
