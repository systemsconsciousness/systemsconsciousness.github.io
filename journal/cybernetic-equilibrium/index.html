    Equilibrium, Feedback, and Mind

    “We are not the stuff that abides, but patterns that perpetuate themselves. A pattern is a message.”

    Norbert Wiener, the father of Cybernetics, recognized that all purposeful organisms, sculptures, and societies sustain their boundaries through balancing negative feedback loops.

    Zen meditation is physical homeostatic regulation in its purest form: noticing divergence, relaxing tension, returning to the breath. When the feedback loop is frictionless, the system enters the state of pure living form.
